1. Introduction

SMART GRADE is a complete Progressive Web Application (PWA) for grade management, designed specifically for SIN GBHS FOUMBAN Form 4B Science students. The application works 100% offline after first installation and stores all data locally on your device.

Key Features Overview

  • 14 subjects with customizable coefficients (1-10)
  • 6 sequences per year (2 per term)
  • 3 terms per academic year
  • 23 achievement badges
  • 20 color themes and 12 font families
  • Night mode (automatic 8pm to 6am)
  • Biometric fingerprint login
  • Export to PDF, CSV, HTML, JSON
  • QR code data transfer between devices
  • Flashcards and goal setting
  • Interactive charts and statistics
Tip: After first installation, you can use SMART GRADE without internet connection.

2. Create an Account

  1. From the Home page, tap the menu button in the top left corner
  2. Select Register
  3. Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your school records
  4. Select your class (B1 or B2)
  5. Enter your unique student number (1 to 70)
  6. Create a secure 4-digit PIN - this is your password
  7. Confirm your PIN by typing it again
  8. Click the Create Account button
Important: Your PIN cannot be recovered if lost. Write it down in a safe place.

3. Login Methods

PIN Method

  • Go to the Login page
  • Select your name from the list
  • Enter your 4-digit PIN
  • Click Sign In

Fingerprint Method

  • First register your fingerprint in Settings
  • On the Login page, tap the fingerprint icon
  • Place your finger on the sensor
  • You will be automatically logged in
Tip: Fingerprint login is faster and more secure. Enable it in Settings.

4. Subjects and Coefficients

Each term can have different subjects. You must select between 10 and 14 subjects per term.

  1. Go to the Settings page
  2. Select Term 1, Term 2, or Term 3
  3. Tap on subject cards to select or deselect them
  4. Adjust coefficients using the + and - buttons (range 1 to 10)
  5. Click Save Subjects when done
The 14 Subjects: Computer Sciences, Mathematics, Chemistry, Human Biology, Geology, Physics, Additional Mathematics, Biology, Economics, English Language, Geography, Citizenship, French, Food and Nutrition.
Tip: The coefficient represents how important the subject is for your final average. Most subjects use coefficient 5.

5. Add Your Grades

  1. Go to the Add Grade page
  2. Select the term (Term 1, 2, or 3)
  3. For each subject, enter your grades in the input fields
  4. Grades must be between 0 and 20 (decimals allowed, e.g., 14.5)
  5. The term average updates automatically as you type
  6. Click Save All Grades when done
Tip: Grades are auto-saved every 30 seconds while you type.

6. Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard is your main hub with:

  • Motivation Card: Inspirational quote to start your day
  • Stats Grid: Shows overall average, total grades, subjects count, and badges
  • Goal Card: Set your target average using the slider (10-20)
  • Term Averages: Shows your average for Term 1, Term 2, Term 3
  • Yearly Average: Combined average of all three terms
  • Top Subjects: Your best performing subjects
  • Recent Activity: Latest actions in your account
  • Command Interface: Type commands like "export", "transfer", "theme"
Tip: Click any Term Card to see the detailed report for that term.

9. Data Transfer

Transfer your data between devices:

  1. Go to the Transfer page
  2. Select QR Code Transfer or Local Transfer
  3. Click Generate Code or Generate QR Code
  4. On the other device, go to Import tab
  5. Scan the QR code or enter the 6-digit code
  6. The data will be imported immediately
Important: Transfer codes expire after 10 minutes for security.

13. Goals and Streaks

Set academic goals and track your progress:

  1. On the Dashboard, use the Goal Card slider to set your target yearly average (10-20)
  2. Go to the Goals page to set subject-specific targets
  3. Each subject has a slider to set your target grade
  4. A progress bar shows how close you are to your target
  5. The app tracks your daily usage for streak badges
Tip: Set realistic goals and watch your progress grow. The app notifies you when you achieve a goal!

14. Grade Calculator

Plan your studies by calculating what grade you need on your next exam:

  1. Go to Settings and scroll down to Grade Calculator
  2. Select a subject
  3. Enter your current average
  4. Enter your target average
  5. Enter the coefficient of your next exam
  6. The calculator shows you exactly what score you need
Formula: Needed Grade = (Target × Total Coeff - Current × Current Coeff) ÷ Exam Coeff
Tip: Use this to know what grade you need to achieve your goal!

15. Export and Backup

Save and restore your data:

  • JSON Backup: Complete backup of all data (grades, badges, profile, flashcards)
  • CSV Export: Spreadsheet format for Excel
  • HTML Bulletin: Viewable report card in your browser
  • PDF Bulletin: Professional PDF file you can download or print
  • Import JSON: Restore your data from a backup file
Important: Make a backup each term to avoid losing your data. Export before clearing browser cache.

19. Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

  • App not loading: Clear browser cache and reload
  • Data not saving: Check localStorage permissions
  • Fingerprint not working: Use PIN login instead
  • QR code not scanning: Ensure good lighting and hold steady
  • PDF not generating: Check internet connection for first use
  • Theme not applying: Refresh the page after selection
  • Grades not showing: Verify subjects are selected in Settings
Recovery: Always export your data as JSON backup regularly.
