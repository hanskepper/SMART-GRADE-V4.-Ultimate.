Frequently Asked Questions
How do I recover my PIN?
For security reasons, PINs cannot be recovered. You will need to create a new account. We recommend saving your PIN in a secure place.
How do I transfer my data?
Go to Transfer page. Generate a 6-digit code or QR code on your source device. On the new device, enter the code or scan the QR code. Codes expire in 10 minutes.
How do backups work?
When Auto-Backup is enabled, your data is saved every hour. You can also create manual backups. All backups are stored locally on your device.
How to change theme or font?
Tap the Theme button in the top right corner. Choose from 20 color themes and 12 different fonts. Night mode activates automatically from 8 PM to 6 AM.
Fingerprint login not working?
Make sure your device has fingerprint hardware and you have registered it in Settings. Use PIN login as an alternative.
